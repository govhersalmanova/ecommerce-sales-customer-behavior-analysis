PROJECT SUMMARY
---------------
Dataset: Synthetic e-commerce order data for Azerbaijan, covering 2025.
Rows: 3,000

KEY KPIs
--------
Total Net Revenue: 407,175.17 AZN
Total Profit: 133,513.24 AZN
Average Order Value: 135.73 AZN
Overall Return Rate: 7.67%
Average Customer Rating: 4.27/5

TOP INSIGHTS
------------
1. Electronics generated the highest net revenue.
2. Baku was the strongest city by net revenue.
3. Fashion had the highest product return rate.
4. Longer delivery times were associated with lower customer ratings.
5. Card payments were the most frequently used payment method.

BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
------------------------
1. Focus marketing and inventory planning on the strongest categories and cities.
2. Review product quality, sizing information, and descriptions in high-return categories.
3. Improve delivery performance, especially for orders taking 6+ days.
4. Create retention campaigns for repeat customers.
5. Track revenue, profit, return rate, and customer satisfaction monthly.
